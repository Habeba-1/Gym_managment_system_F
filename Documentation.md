Gym Management System (GMS)1. OverviewA multi-tenant SaaS Gym Management System targeted at small and medium-sized gyms. The system aims to manage members, subscriptions, attendance, staff, expenses, store, and reports through a single dashboard.ObjectiveProvide a simple and fast system that helps gym owners and receptionists to:Manage member data and subscriptions.Register attendance quickly even with poor connectivity.Check member status and communicate with them.Monitor expenses, revenues, and net profit.Manage staff, shifts, store, and equipment.Isolate each gym's data completely from other gyms.Primary UsersRoleUsage & PermissionsOwnerFull access to gym data, members, subscriptions, reports, expenses, store, staff, and settings.   ReceptionistManage members, subscriptions, attendance, and communication. Restricted from accessing sensitive financial data and restricted settings.   The system does not support self-signup for users. Users are created or invited by the gym owner or system administrator.2. Problems Solved by the SystemSmall and medium gyms typically suffer from:Manual or unorganized subscription management.Difficulty in knowing currently present members.Lack of alerts for new members, expiring subscriptions, or inactive/dropped-out members.Lack of clear tracking for revenues, expenses, and net profit.Separation of store, equipment, and staff management from core operations.Difficulty maintaining data during internet outages.3. Project Scope3.1 MVP ScopeThe core MVP consists of Features 0 to 4:Authentication & Authorization.Members & Subscriptions.Attendance.Classification & Communication.Main Dashboard.3.2 Expansion FeaturesFeatures 5 to 10 represent operational and financial expansion:Staff & Shifts.Operational Expenses.Comprehensive Financial Report.Internal Store.Equipment & Maintenance.Administrative Reminders.In the current execution plan, all Features (0 to 10) will be completed within two weeks, implementing core priorities first, followed by integration and testing.4. Multi-tenancy & Data IsolationThe system is a SaaS serving multiple gyms on the same application. Therefore:   Every operational table must contain gym_id directly or through a clear relationship.   gym_id is extracted from the authenticated user after login.   The client does not send gym_id as a trusted standalone reference; the backend enforces it from the session.   Every query must filter by gym_id.   An owner from one gym cannot access data from another gym.   Isolation must be applied at the API level, not just the frontend.   8.
